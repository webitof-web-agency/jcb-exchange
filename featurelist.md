1. Platform Overview
JCB Exchange ek fully responsive, SEO-friendly aur PWA-enabled used JCB and construction equipment marketplace hoga.
Platform par:
Normal customer vehicle list nahi kar sakega
Sirf admin-approved agents, brokers aur dealers listing add kar sakenge
Partner registration ke baad KYC aur security deposit verification hoga
Har listing publish hone se pehle admin approval mandatory hoga
Customer location, model, price aur condition ke basis par listings search kar sakega
Customer listing owner ya admin se call, WhatsApp aur enquiry ke through contact kar sakega
Contact details admin-controlled rules ke according show hongi
Sold listings ka media 30 din baad automatically delete hoga
Platform SEO, AI search, analytics, notifications aur lead management support karega
